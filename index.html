Overview details

JEPAs learn latent world models without pixel reconstruction. LeWAM trains representation, dynamics, and stochastic action modeling together with a shared predictor and SIGReg.

The model supports both direct policy execution and visual planning. In ten simulated tasks, contact-rich success is 85.5% versus 26.8%; at a 100-step goal offset it is 46.2% versus 10.9%. Planning is 32.3× faster than LeWM (CEM) across four tasks. Goal-free behavior cloning and physical-robot control are evaluated separately.

Joint Learning from Pixels

A shared predictor learns dynamics and a stochastic policy from the same visual representation.

Architecture details

History, future, and goal images share an encoder. A mixture of transformers (MoT) uses separate state and action transformations with masked attention. In simulation, the encoder and predictor train jointly from scratch with a 192-dimensional latent state.

Clean actions condition future-state prediction; noisy actions learn a velocity field through flow matching. SIGReg regularizes the latent distribution.

ℒLeWAM = ℒdyn + ℒact + λsig ℒSIGReg

The state query reads history and clean actions. Goal and remaining-horizon conditioning enter the action readout only, so dynamics stay goal-independent. The physical-robot study also evaluates a frozen pretrained visual backbone; models are trained separately for each task and embodiment.

Direct Control and Planning

Act with the policy, or sample and refine plans through the learned dynamics.

Control and planning details

Direct control. The action flow generates a 2K-action chunk; execute its first K actions, observe again, and repeat.

  1. Sample plans. Alternate stochastic action generation with latent dynamics rollouts.
  2. Refine and select. Optimize candidates through the frozen dynamics using terminal latent goal error and a proximity penalty; choose the lowest-cost iterate.
C(a) = MSE(ẑt+L(a), zg)

Execute the first K planned actions and replan from the next observation.

How to read these videos

The recorded rollout is an actual closed-loop LeWAM policy execution. Both model columns visualize its executed action sequence; the LeWM column is not a separate LeWM policy rollout.

Open-loop prediction starts from initial frames and receives no later ground-truth images. Frame reconstruction encodes and decodes each observed frame. These post-hoc decoders are for visualization only, not control.

Clips are frame-aligned at 10 fps for 5.1 seconds. They are qualitative examples, not success-rate estimates.

Robot evaluation details

The study compares end-to-end LeWAM and a variant with a frozen V-JEPA 2.1 visual backbone against Diffusion Policy and π0.5.

A scene camera and a wrist camera feed a seven-DoF robot policy. A remote inference machine returns action chunks; the robot executes joint targets at 30 Hz. GFLOPs count the full action-chunk inference, including denoising steps.
